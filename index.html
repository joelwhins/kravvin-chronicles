The Wyrmsbane Ambush

November 3, 2026 · Wyrmsbane · Jordain, Human Cleric · Solo vs. approximately 4–5 players
Confirmed kill · Survived · Escaped

Jordain had gone to Wyrmsbane to fight skeletons, not to wage war against other adventurers. Already occupied with several undead foes, he found himself set upon by a group of players. The attackers had the numbers. They had the initiative. They had caught him mid-battle.

They did not, however, have an uncontested victory.

As skeletal weapons rattled against his defenses and hostile magic flashed through the melee, Jordain stood his ground long enough to turn the fight. His blessed barrier absorbed repeated strikes. Holy power answered steel. Augment, one of the attackers, fell beneath Jordain's counterattack.

“Your devotion is rewarded!”
“Your Righteous Strike hits Augment for 36 points of Holy Damage.”
“You have slain Augment!”

Outnumbered and with more enemies still pressing the attack, Jordain chose survival over a second kill. He disengaged and escaped Wyrmsbane alive.

The most memorable blow came after the fight, in a private message from the fallen opponent:

Augment: “how the fuck did you hit me that hard?!”

Stotch also messaged Jordain: “I told them to stop attacking you.” The comment is preserved as a post-battle statement, not proof that Stotch was an attacker.
