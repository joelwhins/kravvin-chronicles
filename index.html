Chapter I · Battle 001

The Wyrmsbane Ambush

November 3, 2026 · Wyrmsbane · Jordain, Level 6 Human Cleric · Approximately 4–5 opponents reported
ONE ENEMY SLAIN · JORDAIN SURVIVED · ESCAPED

The dead were restless in Wyrmsbane, but they were not the greatest danger that day. Jordain, a lone cleric, had been fighting several skeletons when living enemies descended upon him.

Steel and hostile spells came from multiple directions. Slippy struck with piercing weapons and Backstab attempts. Monkeyman's crushing blows joined the assault. Myles unleashed magic. Augment pressed the attack with blade and spell.

Jordain's Blessed Barrier absorbed repeated attacks. Where his enemies expected a quick kill, they met an answer in holy fire.

From the combat log“Your devotion is rewarded!”
“Your Righteous Strike hits Augment for 36 points of Holy Damage.”
“You have slain Augment!”

Augment fell. But the others remained, and Jordain was still outnumbered. Rather than offer them an easy second chance, he broke away and escaped with his life.

It was only afterward that the fallen attacker sent the most memorable line of the encounter.

Augment, in a private message“how the fuck did you hit me that hard?!”

Another message came from Stotch: “I told them to stop attacking you.” Whether Stotch had participated in the fight is unconfirmed. The message stands as a witness's post-battle comment, not evidence of an attack.
