The Kravvin Chronicles · Chapter I

A Prayer for Augment

The Wyrmsbane Ambush
November 3, 2026 · Wyrmsbane · Jordain, Level 6 Human Cleric · Approximately 4–5 opponents reported
ONE ENEMY SLAIN · JORDAIN SURVIVED · ESCAPED

The dead were restless in Wyrmsbane, but they were not the greatest danger that day. Jordain, a lone cleric, had been fighting several skeletons when living enemies descended upon him.

Steel and hostile spells came from multiple directions. Slippy struck with piercing weapons and Backstab attempts. Monkeyman's crushing blows joined the assault. Myles unleashed magic. Augment pressed the attack with blade and spell.

Jordain's Blessed Barrier absorbed repeated attacks. Where his enemies expected a quick kill, they met an answer in holy fire.

From the combat log“Your devotion is rewarded!”
“Your Righteous Strike hits Augment for 36 points of Holy Damage.”
“You have slain Augment!”

Augment fell. But the others remained, and Jordain was still outnumbered. Rather than offer them an easy second chance, he broke away and escaped with his life.

It was only afterward that the fallen attacker sent the most memorable line of the encounter.

Augment, in a private message“how the fuck did you hit me that hard?!”

Another message came from Stotch: “I told them to stop attacking you.” Whether Stotch had participated in the fight is unconfirmed. The message stands as a witness's post-battle comment, not evidence of an attack.

The combat log confirms one kill against Augment, repeated Blessed Barrier absorptions, Righteous Strike for 36 holy damage, Holy Strike for 14–15 holy damage, and a 24-damage crushing hit. The Kravvin Chronicles · Chapter II

A Helping Hand to the Enemy

The Thorns Incident
November 4, 2026 · Outside Night Harbor's West Gate, near the archaeological ruins · Jordain, Level 6 Human Cleric · Thorns, Wood Elf Druid
JORDAIN SLAIN · THORNS VICTORIOUS

Jordain had prepared to meet his enemies with righteous fury. Unfortunately, he had also brought medical assistance, and on this occasion he administered it to the enemy.

Outside Night Harbor's West Gate, near the archaeological ruins, Jordain was already engaged with a blister beetle when Thorns, a Wood Elf Druid, attacked. The beetle kept biting while the druid layered on Swarm of Mites, Winter's Chill, Snare attempts, and a damage shield. Between poison, cold, and the beetle's teeth, there was little room to recover.

Jordain answered with Holy Strike, landing two confirmed blows against Thorns for 13 points of Holy damage each. Then came the fateful mistake. He began casting Lesser Heal without first switching his target back to himself.

The combat log's cruel revelation“Your Lesser Heal heals Thorns for 28 Health.”
“Your Lesser Heal heals Thorns for 28 Health.”

Fifty-six points of healing went straight to the druid trying to kill him. Jordain even cast Cure Disease on Thorns. Some of his other healing attempts were interrupted by the beetle, while Thorns continued applying pressure. The cleric was not merely losing the exchange. For two successful heals, he was actively restoring his opponent.

Thorns ultimately prevailed. The final message left no doubt: “You have been slain by Thorns!”

The names recorded
JordainLevel 6 Human Cleric of Relle. Landed Holy Strikes, inadvertently healed his opponent twice, and was slain.
ThornsWood Elf Druid. Confirmed PvP attacker and victor. Used Swarm of Mites, Winter's Chill, Snare attempts, and a damage shield.
Blister beetleHostile creature Jordain was fighting when attacked. Its bites and Blistering Rot compounded the danger and interrupted heals.
The lesson written in blood

Before casting a heal, explicitly target yourself and verify the target. A cleric who heals the wrong combatant can turn a survivable fight into a gift-wrapped victory for the other side.

Jordain's field note“Always check who you're healing.”
Open factual battle record

Screenshots supplied November 4, 2026 show two successful Lesser Heals on Thorns for 28 HP each (56 HP total), Cure Disease on Thorns, two Holy Strikes on Thorns for 13 holy damage each, hostile Swarm of Mites and Winter's Chill damage, repeated beetle attacks, interrupted healing casts, and the final death message naming Thorns. The location is identified by Jordain's firsthand account; Thorns's level is not confirmed. This narrative dramatizes the documented encounter.
